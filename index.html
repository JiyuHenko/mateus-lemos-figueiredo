How I work

Speed matters. Proof matters more.

I like fast iteration, but I don't treat activity as evidence of correctness. The workflow should tell us what changed, what was proven and what remains uncertain.

01

Understand before editing.

Read the system, repository state and real constraints before proposing the change.

02

Bound authority and side effects.

Agents and integrations should have explicit capabilities, not vague permission to “do whatever is needed.”

03

Validate semantics, not just status.

A green test or successful API call only matters when it proves the actual contract.

04

Turn recurring failures into tooling.

If a mistake repeats, make it a rule, check, template or architectural constraint.

05

Customer success beats unnecessary scope.

The right technical answer can be to simplify, change direction or advise a customer not to buy something they don't need.
